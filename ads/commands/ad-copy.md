Write ad copy variants for a service and an offer.

1. Read $ARGUMENTS: the service, the business and city, the offer, and the platform (Google Ads or Facebook and Instagram). If the offer is missing, ask for one or suggest one before writing.
2. Confirm the call to action: book, call, send a message, or claim the offer.
3. For Google Ads, write two ad variants: three headlines and two descriptions each, with the service and city in the headline.
4. For Facebook and Instagram, write two copy variants: a hook line under 125 characters, supporting text, a headline under 40 characters, and a call to action. Add one creative idea per variant.
5. Check every claim against what the user provided. Never invent a price, a discount, or a result.
6. Present the variants side by side with a one line note on which audience or search each one fits.
