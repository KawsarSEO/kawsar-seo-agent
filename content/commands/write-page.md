# /content:write-page

Writes a ready to publish service page for a treatment and city.

## When invoked with $ARGUMENTS

$ARGUMENTS is a treatment and a city, optionally with a business name or website URL, for example `Lip Filler, Fort Worth` or `Botox, Fort Worth, Radiance Med Spa`.

Follow these steps:

1. Confirm the inputs. If the user gave a website URL, open the live site and take the real facts from it: business name, services, prices, review count, credentials, staff names. Never invent a price, a review count, or a credential. List any fact the business owner still needs to confirm.
2. Name the search intent in one line: book, compare, learn, or find near me. One page answers one intent.
3. Pick one primary keyword, the treatment plus the city, and its natural variations.
4. Write the page in this structure:
   - Title: service plus city, under 60 characters.
   - Meta description: under 160 characters, names the benefit and the call to action.
   - Main heading with the treatment and city.
   - Direct answer first: 40 to 60 words answering the page's main question, right under the heading.
   - Detail sections with clear headings: what the treatment is, who it suits, what it costs or the starting price, what to expect, how long it takes, aftercare.
   - Proof: real reviews, numbers, and credentials only.
   - One call to action: book, call, or ask a question.
   - FAQ: 3 to 5 real questions with short direct answers, plus matching FAQPage schema.
5. Follow the writing rules: short sentences and short paragraphs, facts over adjectives, the city named naturally with real local detail, no keyword stuffing, and plain language a business owner and a client can both read.
6. Check the draft before delivering: title under 60 characters, meta description under 160 characters, direct answer 40 to 60 words, one H1 only, every fact verified, one clear call to action, internal links to related service pages, FAQ schema where the page has real questions.
7. Deliver ready to publish: title, meta description, full page text with headings, and the FAQ schema, followed by the short list of facts that still need the owner's confirmation.
