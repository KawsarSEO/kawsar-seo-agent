Write a ready cold outreach email from a website audit.

1. Read the audit in $ARGUMENTS. Confirm the business name, city, contact name if given, and the verified issues with their page evidence.
2. If no audit is given, or the issues have no page evidence, ask for the audit before writing anything.
3. Pick the top 2 to 3 issues by business harm, most critical first.
4. Write 3 subject lines, each under 50 characters, personalized to the top issue. Star the recommended one.
5. Draft the email under 150 words: greeting, one line showing you looked at the business, one paragraph with the issues and their business harm, one line about your work with one provable result, a free 1 page audit offer, and a two line sign off. No links.
6. Verify every claim in the email against the audit. Remove anything unverified.
7. Present the subject lines and the email as plain paragraphs, ready to paste, plus the follow up dates: Day 3, Day 7, Day 14.
