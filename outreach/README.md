# Outreach

Win local clients with honest, specific outreach. Turn a verified website audit into a cold email a business owner answers, find the gaps competitors leave open, and target the keywords that bring bookings.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| outreach-email | Write cold outreach emails from a verified audit, with subject lines and a Day 3, 7, 14 follow up cadence |
| competitor-analysis | Compare a business against its top rivals on website, keywords, content, GBP, and reviews |
| keyword-research | Find keywords by intent and winnability: local modifiers, question keywords, competitor keywords |

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/outreach:outreach-email` | Write a ready outreach email from an audit you paste in |

## Usage

```
/outreach:outreach-email Botox clinic in Fort Worth, audit: homepage has no prices, no booking button, 212 five star reviews nowhere on the site
```

The command returns 3 subject lines and a ready email under 150 words, plus the follow up dates.
