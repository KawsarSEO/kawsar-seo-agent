# AEO, GEO, and AI Rank Playbook

How to make a business the answer Google shows, and the source AI engines cite.

## AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

AEO targets Google featured snippets, People Also Ask, and voice answers. The engine wants one short, correct answer it can lift verbatim.

### Rules

1. **One question, one answer.** Each question gets its own heading and one direct answer paragraph of 40 to 60 words immediately below it. No introduction, no buildup, answer first.
2. **Write the way people ask.** Use the exact question as the heading: "How much does Botox cost in Fort Worth?" not "Botox Pricing Information."
3. **Detail comes after the answer.** The short answer first, then the explanation, prices, and nuance below it.
4. **Lists and tables win snippets.** Steps, prices, comparisons, and pros and cons belong in lists or tables.
5. **FAQPage schema** for questions the business genuinely answers. Only mark up content that is visible on the page.
6. **Find the questions first.** Google People Also Ask, autocomplete, and the questions clients actually call and ask. Never invent questions nobody asks.

### AEO checklist per page

- [ ] The main question is a heading on the page.
- [ ] A 40 to 60 word direct answer sits directly under it.
- [ ] Prices, times, and facts are stated as numbers, not adjectives.
- [ ] FAQPage schema is present and matches the visible content.
- [ ] The page answers one intent, not five.

## GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

GEO targets ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other AI answers. AI engines cite sources they trust. Trust comes from clear entities, verifiable facts, and independent confirmation.

### Rules

1. **Be a clear entity.** The same business name, address, phone, services, and owner name everywhere: website, GBP, directories, social profiles. Conflicting facts make AI engines skip you or report the wrong details.
2. **Publish quotable facts.** AI engines cite facts, not adjectives. Prices, years in business, review counts, ratings, credentials, number of patients treated. "4.9 stars from 636 Google reviews" gets cited. "Best med spa in town" does not.
3. **Write in complete, self contained sentences.** Each key fact should survive being lifted out of context. Avoid "as mentioned above" and pronouns without a clear subject.
4. **Keep content fresh.** Update service pages and publish new dated content. AI engines favor sources that are current.
5. **Earn independent mentions.** Reviews, local press, directory listings, and partnerships. AI engines treat third party confirmation as trust.
6. **Reviews are AI signals.** Real reviews on Google and on the site itself feed AI answers about the business. A business with no visible reviews is invisible to AI search too.
7. **Answer comparisons honestly.** "Botox vs Dysport", "HydraFacial vs chemical peel." Comparison content is among the most cited by AI engines.

### GEO checklist per business

- [ ] Name, address, phone, and services are identical on the website, GBP, and top directories.
- [ ] The homepage states quotable facts: rating, review count, years in business, credentials.
- [ ] Service pages answer the real questions clients ask, in direct language.
- [ ] At least one comparison page exists (treatment A vs treatment B).
- [ ] Content has visible dates and is updated regularly.
- [ ] Reviews are visible on the site and answered on Google.

## AI Rank: measuring visibility in AI answers

AI Rank is whether AI engines mention the business when a client asks a buying question, and what they say about it.

### Test template

Run the same prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Record the baseline before any fixes.

| Prompt | Engine | Date | Mentioned? | Position | What it said | Wrong facts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Best med spa in Fort Worth for Botox | ChatGPT | | | | | |
| Best med spa in Fort Worth for Botox | Perplexity | | | | | |
| Best med spa in Fort Worth for Botox | Google AI Overview | | | | | |
| [Business name] reviews | ChatGPT | | | | | |
| [Business name] prices | Perplexity | | | | | |

### Prompt ideas (adapt to the niche and city)

- Best [service] in [city]
- [Business name] reviews
- [Business name] prices
- [Service] near me
- [Service A] vs [Service B] in [city]

### After the test

1. **Not mentioned at all:** entity problem. Fix NAP consistency, add quotable facts, publish service pages, earn reviews and mentions.
2. **Mentioned with wrong facts:** conflicting data in circulation. Find the wrong source (old address, old phone, wrong hours) and correct it at the source.
3. **Mentioned but ranked below competitors:** authority gap. More reviews, fresher content, more independent mentions.
4. **Re-test weekly, not daily.** AI Rank moves slower than Google rank. Record every test so progress is visible.
