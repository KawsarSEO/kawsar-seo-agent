# SEO Audit Checklist

Work through every section on the live site. Record only what you actually observe, with the page and section as evidence. Rank issues by business harm, most costly first.

## 1. Technical

- [ ] Homepage loads over HTTPS. An HTTP site triggers browser warnings before a visitor sees anything.
- [ ] Homepage loads at all. A down or DNS failing site means zero bookings from search.
- [ ] One canonical domain (no live staging subdomain, no duplicate www and non www versions serving the same content).
- [ ] Page titles are unique, under 60 characters, and name the service plus the city.
- [ ] No duplicated blocks on the same page: repeated headings, repeated bios, repeated service sections, repeated testimonials, duplicated navigation.
- [ ] No placeholder content live: Lorem ipsum, John Doe reviews, demo testimonials, "No items found" errors, backend author names or timestamps leaking into visible text.
- [ ] No broken links sending visitors to another business or a dead page.
- [ ] Images have descriptive file names and alt text (no "injectibles" style misspellings in file names).
- [ ] No keyword stuffed titles that Google truncates mid word.

## 2. On page content

- [ ] The homepage names real treatments or services, not only philosophy and biography.
- [ ] Prices or starting prices appear for at least the main services. No prices means shoppers leave to compare elsewhere.
- [ ] Headings carry service keywords and the city. A heading that says only "Home" or "Medspa" cannot rank.
- [ ] Content is complete: no paragraphs cut off mid sentence, no empty sections under a heading.
- [ ] Contact details are present and consistent: one address, one phone number, one email. Two addresses or two phones on one page confuse Google and send clients to the wrong place.
- [ ] No misspelled doctor, owner, or brand names anywhere on the page.
- [ ] Testimonials are real: names, star ratings, and a link to Google reviews. Stock photo testimonials, paraphrased testimonials admitted not to be real quotes, and demo labeled testimonials all cost trust.
- [ ] Reviews appear on the homepage. A business with hundreds of five star reviews and none on its own site wastes its strongest proof.

## 3. Local SEO and Google Business Profile

- [ ] Business name, address, and phone match exactly across the website, GBP, and directories.
- [ ] GBP categories match the real services (med spa, not only the parent clinic or surgery practice).
- [ ] Service pages exist per treatment and per city served. One page per service is the minimum.
- [ ] Reviews are answered, including the unhappy ones.
- [ ] No second location or old address still circulating in directories.
- [ ] Photos on GBP are recent and real, not stock.

## 4. Conversion

- [ ] A booking button or booking link is visible without scrolling.
- [ ] A phone number is visible on every page.
- [ ] A lead capture option exists: contact form, newsletter signup, or free consultation offer.
- [ ] The contact form works: no simultaneous success and error messages, no form that goes nowhere.
- [ ] One clear call to action per page. A page that asks the visitor to download an app, call, and fill a form at once asks for nothing clearly.

## 5. Trust

- [ ] Real staff names, photos, and credentials on the site.
- [ ] No stale seasonal content (a January banner in November, an expired promotion, an old event).
- [ ] No conflicting facts anywhere: two suites, two phones, two spellings of a name.
- [ ] Awards and press mentions are real and dated.

## Framing: issue plus business harm

Every issue is reported in this shape:

**What is wrong:** one sentence, specific, with the page and section.
**What it costs:** lost bookings, lost Google visibility, or lost trust, in the client's own terms.

Pain categories to use: not SEO friendly, content does not attract customers, no call to action, no lead capture option on the page.

## Outreach email template

Use this exact structure for a cold outreach email. Under 150 words. No links.

1. `Hi [First name],`
2. `I looked at [Business] while researching [City] area spas, and spotted a few things costing you bookings.`
3. One paragraph: 2 to 3 specific verified issues with their business harm.
4. `I do Local SEO and Google Business Profile work for med spas and helped one grow Google direction requests by 339 percent.`
5. `Happy to send over a free 1 page audit showing exactly what to fix. Just reply and I will send it over.`
6. Sign off on two lines: `Best regards,` then `Mohammad Kawsar`.

Add 3 subject lines per email, each under 50 characters, personalized to the top issue, with the recommended one starred.
