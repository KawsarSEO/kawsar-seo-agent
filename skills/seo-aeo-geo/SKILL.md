# SEO, AEO, GEO, AI Rank, and Content Writing

A single playbook for making a business findable everywhere clients search: Google, Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.

## When to use this skill

- Website audit for a local business (med spa, clinic, salon, contractor, any local service)
- Local SEO and Google Business Profile work
- Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice answers
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): getting cited by AI search engines
- AI Rank: checking and improving visibility inside AI answers
- Content writing: service pages, blog posts, FAQs, local landing pages

## House rules (always apply)

1. **Verify before claiming.** Open the live site and read the actual page. Only report issues you observed, with the page or section as evidence. Never invent an issue, a number, a price, an email, or a phone number.
2. **Frame every issue with business harm.** Say what it costs: lost bookings, lost Google visibility, lost trust. Lead with the most critical, most costly issue.
3. **Use the client's own terms.** Pain categories a business owner instantly recognizes: not SEO friendly, content does not attract customers, no call to action, no lead capture option on the page. The specific verified issue is the proof; the pain category is what makes them feel it.
4. **No speed or mobile test claims** unless a real test was run.
5. **No invented contact details.** Emails and phone numbers come only from pages the business itself publishes. Flag anything uncertain instead of presenting it as verified.
6. **Recheck a down homepage** before writing about it. A site that fails to load once may be a temporary error.
7. **Plain language.** The reader is a business owner. Write so they can act on it without knowing SEO jargon.

## Workflow 1: SEO audit

1. Open the homepage and the main service pages. Read the real text, not a summary.
2. Work through `references/seo-audit-checklist.md`: technical, on page, local, GBP, reviews, content, conversion.
3. Record each issue with: what it is, where it appears (page and section), and what it costs the business.
4. Rank issues by business harm, most costly first. Pick the top 5 to 8.
5. Deliver: the ranked issue list, the top pitch angle, and the fix for each issue. If outreach is the goal, add 3 subject lines and a ready email under 150 words following the template in the checklist.

## Workflow 2: AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

1. Find the questions clients actually ask: Google People Also Ask, autocomplete, and the business's own FAQ calls.
2. For each question, write one direct answer in 40 to 60 words at the top of the relevant page, then the detail below.
3. Add FAQPage schema for the questions the business genuinely answers.
4. Structure pages so answers are extractable: one clear question heading, one short answer paragraph, then supporting detail.
5. See `references/aeo-geo-playbook.md` for the full checklist.

## Workflow 3: GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

1. Make the business a clear entity: consistent name, address, phone, and service list everywhere (website, GBP, directories).
2. Publish quotable facts: prices, numbers, years in business, review counts, credentials. AI engines cite facts, not adjectives.
3. Keep content fresh: update service pages and publish new dated content regularly.
4. Earn mentions: reviews, local press, directories, and partnerships that AI engines treat as independent confirmation.
5. See `references/aeo-geo-playbook.md` for the full checklist.

## Workflow 4: AI Rank

1. Test visibility: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews the exact questions a client would ask (for example "best med spa in Fort Worth for Botox") and record whether the business appears, and what is said about it.
2. Record the baseline: date, prompt, engine, mentioned or not, position, and any wrong facts.
3. Fix what the test exposes: missing entity data, thin content, no reviews on the site, wrong address or phone in circulation.
4. Re-test after fixes. AI Rank moves slower than Google rank; re-test weekly, not daily.
5. See `references/aeo-geo-playbook.md` for the test template.

## Workflow 5: Content writing

1. Start from search intent: what the searcher wants (book, compare, learn, find near me).
2. One page, one intent, one primary keyword plus its natural variations.
3. Structure: direct answer first (40 to 60 words), then detail, then proof (reviews, numbers, credentials), then one clear call to action.
4. Local pages name the city and neighborhood naturally, with real local detail, never keyword stuffing.
5. See `references/content-writing-guide.md` for the full guide.

## Outputs

- **Audit:** ranked issues with business harm, top pitch angle, fixes.
- **Outreach email:** under 150 words, 2 to 3 specific verified issues, proof line, free 1 page audit offer, soft call to action, no links.
- **Content:** ready to publish page or post, with title, headings, meta description, and FAQ schema where it fits.
- **AI Rank report:** baseline test results, gaps found, fixes applied, re-test date.
