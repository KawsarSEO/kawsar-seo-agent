## Pairs most often confused

- **aeo vs geo.** AEO targets Google featured snippets and voice answers. GEO targets AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Use AEO when the goal is the snippet; use GEO when the goal is the AI citation.
- **seo-content-writing vs human-content.** seo-content-writing writes a new page from scratch for search. human-content rewrites existing AI sounding text so it reads like a person wrote it.
- **website-audit vs competitor-analysis.** website-audit examines the client's own site for issues. competitor-analysis examines rivals to find gaps the client can win.
- **keyword-research vs competitor-analysis.** keyword-research finds what the client should target. competitor-analysis finds what rivals already win. Use keyword-research first, then competitor-analysis to check the gaps.
- **gbp-optimization vs review-management.** gbp-optimization fixes the profile itself. review-management earns and answers the reviews on it.
- **google-ads vs facebook-ads.** google-ads catches people already searching to buy. facebook-ads puts an offer in front of people who match the audience. Use google-ads for demand that exists; use facebook-ads to create it.
- **outreach-email vs website-audit.** website-audit finds the issues. outreach-email turns them into an email. Always audit first, then write.
